The part you never see

Six places a chimney quietly fails.

Hover any point on the cutaway. This is what Junior is actually looking at when he is up there — and what a $199 “special” from a call centre never checks.

01 · The cap

Missing or rusted caps let rain, leaves, squirrels and birds straight into the flue. It is the cheapest part of the whole system and the one that causes the most expensive damage when it is gone.

02 · The crown

The concrete slab on top. Once it cracks, water runs down inside the masonry, freezes, and takes the brickwork apart from within. Most crowns in Bucks County are past due for a rebuild.

03 · The flue liner

The barrier between a 1,000°F fire and the timber framing of your house. Cracked clay tile, gaps in the mortar joints, or a flue oversized for a new furnace all mean the same thing: it needs relining.

04 · The flashing

Where the chimney meets the roof. Nine out of ten “chimney leaks” we get called about are failed flashing or unsealed masonry — not the roof, and not the flue.

05 · The damper & smoke chamber

A seized damper wastes heat all winter and will not close in a chimney fire. A rough, unparged smoke chamber is where creosote loves to build — and where most fires actually start.

06 · The firebox

Spalled firebrick and open mortar joints put heat directly against the structure behind them. It is visible from your living room and almost nobody looks.

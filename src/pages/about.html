The story

Thirty years of learning what a chimney is about to do.

Junior has been working on chimneys and masonry for three decades — long enough to walk up to a stack from the driveway and already have a fair idea of what he is going to find on top of it.

He opened Junior’s Chimney in 2018 to do it his own way: one certified technician on every job, honest pricing quoted up front, and a house left cleaner than he found it. No subcontractors. No pressure. No inventing problems that are not there.

That is why almost every job on the calendar comes from a repeat customer or a neighbour they sent. Real estate agents keep his number for settlements. Insurance adjusters ask for him by name. And people who have been burned by a cheap “$99 special” tend to stay a very long time once they find him.

The equipment

Professional tools, not a shop vac.

The difference between a real sweep and a cheap one is almost entirely in what comes out of the truck.

HEPA extraction

Negative-pressure vacuum at the firebox for the entire job, so fine soot never enters your living space instead of settling on it later.

Rotary flue systems

Powered rotary heads for glazed creosote that a hand brush will not touch — the build-up that actually starts chimney fires.

Inspection camera

Photos and video of the inside of your flue. You see what we see, instead of taking our word for it.

Full masonry kit

Mixers, grinders, scaffold and refractory materials on the truck, so a repair does not become a second appointment three weeks out.

Straight answers

Questions we get asked.

What does a small business site cost?

It is quoted flat, before anything starts, and it depends on how many pages and how much content needs writing. What it never includes is a monthly platform fee — you own the site and host it for free or close to it.

Do I own it, or do you?

You do. The files, the domain, the hosting account and the analytics all end up in your name. We will hand the whole thing over on request, no negotiation.

Can you take over a site somebody else built?

Usually. Send us the link and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth rescuing or whether starting clean costs you less.

How long does it take?

A small business site is typically a week or two once we have your content and photos. Apps and internal tools depend entirely on scope — we will give you a real range after the first conversation, not an optimistic one.

Do you work with people outside Pennsylvania?

Yes. We are based in Bucks County, but almost all of the work happens over email and calls anyway.

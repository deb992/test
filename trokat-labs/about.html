We build the thing we needed first.

TroKat Labs did not start as a company. It started as a way to get through a day.

The origin

A brain injury changes what “simple” means.

After a traumatic brain injury, the ordinary scaffolding of a day — remembering an appointment, taking the right medication at the right hour, knowing what comes next — stops holding itself up. The software that exists assumes you already have the thing you have lost.

So we built something that assumed less and helped more. Not a medical device, not a clinical tool — a daily app that gives back routine, independence and confidence, and lets the people around you help without taking over.

That is still how every TroKat product starts. Someone has a genuine problem, we sit with it long enough to understand it properly, and then we build the smallest honest thing that fixes it.

“Empowering independence. Supporting every step forward.”

What we hold to

  • Built for TBI, useful for everyone.
    Design that works on a hard day works better on an easy one.
  • Your information is yours.
    You stay in control of your data. We do not sell it, ever.
  • Support that includes the people around you.
    Family and carers can help without needing to take the wheel.
  • No jargon.
    If a screen needs explaining, the screen is wrong.

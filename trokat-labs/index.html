Why we exist

It started with one person getting their day back.

TroKat Labs was built around a hard problem: how do you hold a day together after a traumatic brain injury — the appointments, the medications, the routine, the confidence — without depending on somebody else for every step?

The first app existed because it had to. Everything since has been built the same way: start with a real person, a real challenge, and a genuine desire to make life a little better. That is still the whole test a TroKat product has to pass.

Service 01

Chimney Sweeping & Inspection

The job everything else depends on. Rotary brushes and rods through the full flue, a HEPA vacuum running the entire time, and a proper Level 1 inspection of the system from cap to firebox.

What you get

Creosote and soot removed from the full flue, the smoke chamber and the firebox. Damper cleaned and checked for free travel. Cap, crown and flashing inspected. Photos of anything that needs your attention.

How long

About an hour for a standard fireplace. Heavier build-up or a glazed flue takes longer — we will tell you before we start, not after.

The mess

There isn’t any. Drop cloths on the hearth and the floor, HEPA extraction at the source, and a wipe-down before we leave.

Service 02

Chimney Relining

This is Junior’s speciality. When the clay tile inside your chimney has cracked, or the flue is oversized for a newer high-efficiency furnace, the only real answer is a properly sized stainless liner.

When you need one

Cracked or shifted flue tiles. A chimney fire in the history of the house. A furnace, boiler or water heater swapped for a high-efficiency unit. A wood stove insert. Or a failed certification.

Why it matters

The liner is the only thing between combustion gases and the wood framing of your house. A cracked flue leaks carbon monoxide into living space and heat into your walls.

How we do it

Sized to the appliance, insulated where code requires it, top plate and cap installed, and the whole system tested before we call it done. Most jobs finish in a day.

Service 03

Chimney Repairs

Water is what actually destroys chimneys — not fire. Almost every repair we do traces back to something letting rain in and freeze-thaw doing the rest.

A

Crown rebuilds

Cracked or crumbling crowns cut out and recast properly, with an overhang and a drip edge so water sheds instead of soaking in.

B

Flashing & leaks

Step and counter flashing replaced and sealed where the stack meets the roof. Most “roof leaks” near a chimney start right here.

C

Caps & dampers

Stainless caps with spark arrestor mesh, and dampers that actually seal — including top-sealing dampers that stop winter heat loss.

D

Tuckpointing

Failed mortar joints ground out and repointed to match. Done before the brick starts moving, it is a fraction of a rebuild.

E

Waterproofing

Breathable masonry sealer that keeps rain out while letting the chimney dry — the cheapest insurance on the whole stack.

F

Firebox repair

Spalled firebrick replaced and joints repointed with refractory mortar rated for the temperatures it actually sees.

Straight answers

Questions we get every week.

How often does a chimney actually need sweeping?

Once a year for anything you burn regularly — wood, pellet, gas or oil. Wood burners who go through more than a cord a season should have it looked at mid-winter too. If you have not burned in it at all this year, you still want it inspected: most problems we find are water and animal damage, not soot.

What does it cost?

You get a flat price before any work starts, and it does not change unless you agree to something extra. Estimates are free. Pricing depends on the height of the stack, what is being burned and how much build-up is in there — which is why we quote it honestly instead of advertising a bait price.

Do you make a mess?

No. Drop cloths go down before anything else, a HEPA vacuum runs at the firebox the whole time, and the hearth gets wiped down before we leave. Junior has been doing this for thirty years specifically so nobody has to clean up after him.

My chimney is leaking. Is that a roof problem?

Usually not. Nine times out of ten it is failed flashing where the stack meets the roof, a cracked crown, or porous brick that has never been sealed. We will tell you honestly if it is actually your roof — we would rather send you to a roofer than sell you a repair that does not fix it.

Can you do a certification before my settlement?

Yes, and quickly. Tell us your closing date when you call. You get a written, signed certification with photos, and if anything fails you get a quote to correct it in the same visit so the deal keeps moving.

Are you insured?

Fully insured, and happy to send a certificate of insurance to you, your agent or your township before we start. Every cleaning is performed by a Certified Chimney Professional technician.
